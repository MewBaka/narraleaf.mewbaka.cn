---
title: "VideoFadeOptions"
description: Duration and easing for Video.show and Video.hide.
---
```typescript
type VideoFadeOptions = {
    duration?: number;
    easing?: TransformDefinitions.EasingDefinition;
};
```

Passed to [`Video.show`](/docs/narraleaf-react/core/elements/video#show) or [`Video.hide`](/docs/narraleaf-react/core/elements/video#hide). With no positive `duration`, the clip appears or disappears at once.

`easing` accepts a named easing or a cubic-bezier array. It is linear when omitted. A fade starts when the video can present a frame, and the action waits for the fade to finish. Available since `1.1.0`.
