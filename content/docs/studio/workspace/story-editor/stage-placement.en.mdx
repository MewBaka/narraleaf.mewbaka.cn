---
title: Placing objects on stage
description: Drag and resize a character or image from the story preview, with snapping, proportional resize and one undo step per gesture.
---

With the cursor on a row that shows or moves a character or an image, the story preview draws a box around that object. Dragging the box or a corner rewrites the position and size stated by the row.

## Move

- Drag the box to move the object
- Its centre snaps to the stage's middle and thirds
- Its edges snap to the stage edges and safe area
- Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to keep to one axis
- Hold <kbd>Cmd</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> to move without snapping
- The magnet button in the preview header turns snapping off

## Resize

- Drag a corner to resize
- The opposite corner stays in place and proportions are kept
- Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to resize each axis separately
- Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> to resize about the centre

The row is written when the pointer is released, and one drag is one undo step. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels the drag.

A row that only hides an object, changes an expression or plays a motion cannot be placed. The preview names the reason.
