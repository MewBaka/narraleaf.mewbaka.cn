title: Stage timeline
description: Read the stage after every row of a scene, follow branches, and jump from a lane or column back to its row.
---

The **Stage Timeline** in the bottom dock shows the stage after every row of the current scene, in execution order. Each supported object has one lane, and each row has one column. The ruler numbers dialogue and narration rows only.

<Figure
    src="/static/img/docs/studio/en/stage-timeline.webp"
    alt="The stage timeline in the bottom panel: dialogue, background, a character and music each get a track, and the selected column is the scene editor's current row"
/>

Lanes cover:

- the background
- characters
- images and text objects
- ambience effects
- the camera
- music and sound

An object that never appears in the scene has no lane.

## Reading the lanes

A bar means the object is on stage. A new expression or picture starts a new block. A change that keeps the object's name, such as a move or a zoom, appears as a diamond on the object it changes.

Each dialogue line is as wide as its words. A row that only acts on the stage keeps a narrow fixed width. The timeline fills the panel when the panel is widened or made taller.

## Navigation and branches

Select a column to move the scene editor and preview to that row.

At a menu, the timeline follows the first option by default. Select the branch button on the ruler to follow another option, or place the cursor inside an option to expand that branch.

Conditions are evaluated the way the preview evaluates them. Rows after a jump, an ending or a quit are not shown unless the path can reach them.

## Zoom

Use the zoom slider at the top, or hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> / <kbd>Cmd</kbd> and scroll. **Show all row numbers** labels every row on the ruler.
